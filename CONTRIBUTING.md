# Contributing

- **A new edge case:** add a synthetic deal to `code/data/opportunities.json` and a test in `code/tests/test_onboarding.py`. Never add a real customer.
- **A new checklist item:** add it to `code/rules.json` and a row in `docs/closed-won-checklist.md`. Rules are examples. Say who owns the item.
- **A Salesforce, NetSuite or Stacksync detail changed:** update `docs/salesforce-netsuite-notes.md` and name the page and date you checked.
- Describe behavior, not outcomes. No savings, accuracy or compliance claims you cannot source. No regulatory statements about tax numbers.
- Use synthetic data only. Never include real customers, tax numbers, credentials or internal URLs.
